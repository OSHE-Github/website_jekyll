More About Our Agency.

The EERC (pronounced "erk") holds the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. The ECE department is directly tied to the success of OSHE students. We are one of the largest enterprises on campus, made of primarily Electrical Engineers, Computer Engineers, and Robotics Engineers. Do not be discouraged to join if you are not in the ECE department, we have hosted a plethora of student degrees, including (but not limited to) Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Mechatronics, and more!

Historically, OSHE's lab has been a hub for innovative research and development in the field of electrical and computer engineering. Soon, the lab will be migrating from the DOW building to the EERC for a more integrated ECE experience.

With Dr. Shane Oberloier as our advisor and with our dedicated e-board, OSHE is a great enterprise for any major and any level of student. We are here to make you industry ready through the passionate lens of open-source hardware.
