#### OSTR (Open Source Thunniform Robot)
OSTR is a biomimetic underwater robot based on thunniform swimming. The project combines a redesigned body and waterproofing with buoyancy control, yaw steering, wired or wireless operation, sensor interfaces, and an onboard depth-sensing camera. The CAD, Raspberry Pi HAT, and ROS 2 workspace are maintained in the repositories linked under Website.
