#### Deer Dissuader
Deer Dissuader is a deer detection and deterrence system. It uses a Raspberry-Pi 3B+ running OpenCV to detect a deer in the camera's vicinity. When detected, it will sound a preprogrammed noise (humans talking) to scare off the deer. Practical uses include at-home gardens, road-sides, and backyards.
