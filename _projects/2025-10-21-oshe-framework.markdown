#### OSHE Framework
Framework is an open source laptop and desktop brand focusing on the right to repair. Our goal is to build off this framework and develop ideas for the community based on what we feel is both wanted and applicable to the current modules. Keeping Framework’s standard design constraints, which include the casing size for the expansion cards and the front panels slot sizes, we have created a variety of projects including an I2C/UART expansion card, an LCD front panel, an updated OLED Display, a Magnetic chagrin adapter, and a USB dongle hider expansion card. Designing these devices and outreach to the Framework community was the main goal of this project.
