#### BREAD Local Software
BREAD (Broadly Reconfigurable and Expandable Automation Device) is an open-source hardware and software platform designed for rapid system development and integration. It allows for custom “slices” that connect into a central “loaf,” enabling modular control and monitoring of complex systems. Slices are developed using open-source and easily accessible materials and software. This platform is very flexible and can interface with many inputs and outputs. 
The BREAD BiOS project focuses on enhancing the existing BREAD-based automation system integrated into the chem department’s bioreactor. This bioreactor converts waste plastic to protein through a chemical process. Precise environmental control is critical to the success of these processes.
