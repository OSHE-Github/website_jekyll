#### MultiFX
The MultiFX Pedal is an open-source, modular multi-effects processor built around a Raspberry Pi. It integrates a control PCB with three rotary encoders, a footswitch, a HiFiBerry-class DAC HAT for audio I/O, and a Python/PyQt5 GUI that controls audio effects via MOD-host and JACK.
