#### Husky Clean
Husky Clean is an autonomous floor-cleaning robot for spaces such as laboratories, commercial facilities, and restaurants. The design dispenses cleaning solution, scrubs floors, and vacuums up residual water, with robotics vision and lidar supporting navigation. The reports acknowledge GM EMI and SICK as project sponsors.
