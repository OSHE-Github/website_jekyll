#### Modular Gantry Toolhead Platform
This project extends the [OSHE Pick-and-Place platform](https://github.com/OSHE-Github/OSHE-PnP) with standardized mechanical, electrical, and work-surface interfaces. The first semester specification targets adapting the existing toolhead and prototyping at least one additional interchangeable toolhead.
